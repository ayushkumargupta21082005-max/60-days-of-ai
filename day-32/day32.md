## 🎯 The Learning Path

### Step 1: Choose Your Starting Point
Pick one of three paths:
- **🏢 Use My Own Business** — Develop strategy for your real business
- **🙋 Build My Personal Brand** — Create a personal brand using your expertise and story
- **🎲 Random Client** — Learn with a randomly generated business (Sustainable Fashion, AI Fitness App, Coffee Roastery, etc.)

### Step 2: Understand Your Audience
Answer the questions that matter:
- **What are their pain points?** What keeps them up at night?
- **What are their goals?** What do they want to achieve?
- **Where do they spend time?** LinkedIn? TikTok? Discord? Podcasts?

💡 **Why It Matters**: Brands that speak to real problems win. Ignore this and your marketing will feel like spam.

### Step 3: Choose Your Platforms (2-4)
Pick where you'll actually show up:
- Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Twitter/X, Newsletter, Discord, Threads, etc.

Each platform has different audiences and different content rules. A TikTok strategy doesn't work on LinkedIn.

💡 **Why It Matters**: Beginners try to be everywhere and burn out. Pros pick 2-4 platforms and own them completely.

### Step 4: Pick Your Content Pillars (Exactly 3)
Choose the 3 core themes you'll create content about:
- Thought Leadership, Personal Story, Behind-the-Scenes, Education, Case Studies, Hot Takes, Product Showcase, Customer Stories, Trends, etc.

These three pillars form the backbone of everything you create. They keep you consistent and help your audience know what to expect.

💡 **Why It Matters**: Random content gets forgotten. Strategic content builds authority. Consistency beats virality every time.

### Step 5: Build Your 30-Day Roadmap
See your 4-week game plan:
- **Week 1**: Launch & Define — Clarify your message, optimize your profile
- **Week 2**: Start Teaching — Post insights, engage, get feedback
- **Week 3**: Build Community — Share stories, respond, collaborate
- **Week 4**: Analyze & Repeat — See what worked, double down

💡 **Why It Matters**: Strategy without a plan is just talk. This roadmap shows you exactly what to do each week.

### Step 6: Handle a Marketing Event
Reality throws curveballs. You get a real scenario:
- Your post goes viral — Do you respond to comments or promote your service?
- Someone copies your content — Do you call them out or DM privately?
- A competitor undercuts your price — Do you match or lean into unique value?

You make a choice. You see the consequences.

💡 **Why It Matters**: New marketers panic when things don't go according to plan. This teaches you how to respond wisely under pressure.

### Step 7: Get Your Growth Report
See your complete strategy summarized:
- Your brand, audience, platforms, and pillars
- 3 essential marketing lessons
- Next steps for building your 90-day plan

## 📚 What You'll Learn

### Three Core Marketing Lessons

**1. Consistency Beats Virality**
- You don't need 1M followers. You need 1,000 true fans who trust you.
- Show up weekly. For a year. That's it. There's no secret.
- One post that flops is fine. Ghosting for a month kills everything.

**2. Niche Down to Scale Up**
- The biggest mistake is trying to appeal to everyone.
- Pick a specific audience (women in tech, founders in B2B, designers in startups).
- Be specific, and you own that space.

**3. Authenticity Wins**
- People follow real people, not brands.
- Your vulnerability is your superpower.
- The best personal brands are honest about their journey.

### How to Ask Claude Prompts

After every major section, you'll get a **"How to Ask Claude"** card with a copy-paste prompt you can use in ChatGPT or Claude.

Learn prompt engineering while learning marketing:

- Ask Claude to analyze a competitor's strategy
