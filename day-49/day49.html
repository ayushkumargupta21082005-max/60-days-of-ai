How to Use Your AI Playbook

📊 Dashboard

Your stats and quick access to featured workflows. As you build and save workflows, stats update automatically.

🧰 Prompt Builder

Combine building blocks (Role, Objective, Context, etc.) to create powerful debugging prompts. Each block explains what it does. See a live preview on the right—copy it or save it as a workflow.

♻️ Loop Builder

Turn any prompt into an iterative debugging loop. Define success criteria, evaluation methods, and stop conditions. AI will refine its answers automatically.

💾 My Workflows

Save your best prompts and loops here. Favorite them, duplicate them, edit them. Everything is stored locally on your device.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

Ctrl+B - Prompt Builder
Ctrl+L - Loop Builder
Ctrl+S - Save current workflow
Ctrl+? - Help (this modal)

All your data is stored locally. No internet required after initial load. Export anytime to back up your workflows.
