Scoring Methodology

Each laptop is scored 0-100 on each criterion:

  • Price: Inverse scale (lower = better). $999 = 100pts, scales down.
  • Performance: Highest Geekbench score = 100pts, others scale proportionally.
  • Battery: Longest battery life = 100pts, scales proportionally.
  • Display: Combined resolution + brightness score = 100pts max.
  • Portability: Lightest weight = 100pts, scales with weight increase.

Final Score Calculation: Weighted sum of all criteria using your slider adjustments.

⚠️ Data Freshness: All specifications verified for current models as of August 2026. Prices and performance benchmarks may vary by region and configuration. No estimates were used—all figures are from official sources or verified tech benchmarks.
