Key Learning Points
How to Use the Prior Authorization Simulator

Objective: Guide patients through the healthcare prior authorization workflow by clicking through stages in the correct order.

Workflow: Follow three lanes: Patient (insurance setup), Provider (medical review and documentation), and Payer (authorization decision).

Documents: Collect all required documents before submission to the payer.

Decisions: When you reach the Decision stage, choose Approve, Pend, Deny, or Appeal based on the clinical evidence presented.

Scoring: Your efficiency score decreases with mistakes. Complete scenarios quickly with high accuracy!

Learning: After each stage, read the educational card to understand real-world healthcare processes.
