📖 How to Play

Objective
You are the Head of Operations. Manage supply chain incidents within 3 minutes to maximize your score and operational performance.
Game Mechanics
  • Alerts - Supply chain incidents appear randomly. Each has a priority level (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
  • Time Pressure - You have limited time. Each alert has a countdown timer
  • Decisions Matter - Your choices affect KPIs and future risk
  • Consequences - Some decisions have delayed effects that appear later
KPI Indicators
  • Service Level - On-time delivery %; should stay above 90%
  • Customer Satisfaction - Overall satisfaction score
  • Inventory Health - Warehouse optimization levels
  • Transport Efficiency - Logistics performance
  • Operating Cost - Lower is better
  • Revenue Protected - Prevented losses from incidents
Strategy Tips
  • Prioritize Critical alerts first
  • Don't ignore problems - they escalate
  • Balance speed with cost - expensive solutions may hurt KPIs
  • Keep inventory healthy to prevent cascading failures
  • Good decisions = higher score + better grade
