# 🛡️ Featured Project — Defend Your Experience

One of the projects in this challenge is **Defend Your Experience**.

It is an AI-powered interview preparation application designed to help users confidently defend the claims they make about their experience.

### 💡 How it works

```text
User Experience / Resume
          ↓
    Claim Extraction
          ↓
   Claude AI Analysis
          ↓
   Tough Interview Question
          ↓
      User Answer
          ↓
   Claude Assessment
          ↓
 Defensibility Score
          ↓
 Follow-up Question
          ↓
   Final Report
```

### ✨ Key Features

* 📄 Upload or enter experience information
* 🎯 Extract important claims
* 🤖 Generate personalized interview questions
* 💬 Adaptive follow-up questions
* 📊 Defensibility scoring
* ⚠️ Identify weak claims
* 📈 Track interview progress
* 📋 Generate final defensibility report
* 📥 Export interview report
* 🎨 Multiple interface styles

### 🤖 Claude Integration

Claude is used for:

1. Extracting key claims from experience
2. Generating challenging interview questions
3. Assessing candidate answers
4. Providing feedback
5. Generating deeper follow-up questions
