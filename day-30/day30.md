# 🌍 Supply Chain Builder

> Learn supply chain management by building one yourself. **Supply Chain Builder** is an interactive educational simulator that teaches supply chain design through real-world business decisions, live metrics, and beginner-friendly explanations.

---

# 📌 Overview

**Supply Chain Builder** is a browser-based interactive learning application that transforms complex supply chain concepts into an engaging simulation.

Instead of reading theory, users build a complete supply chain by making strategic business decisions and immediately seeing how those choices impact cost, delivery speed, customer satisfaction, sustainability, and operational risk.

Every decision includes simple explanations, helping beginners understand **what the concept means, why it matters, and its real-world business impact.**

---

# ✨ Features

## 🏢 Random Company Generator

Every playthrough creates a unique company including:

- Industry
- Products
- Countries Served
- Annual Revenue
- Demand Level

This ensures every simulation is different.

---

## 📚 Beginner-Friendly Learning

Before every decision, the simulator explains:

- What the concept means
- Why it matters
- Real-world business impact
- Common industry trade-offs

Designed specifically for students and beginners.

---

## 🏭 Build Your Supply Chain

Make strategic decisions across five major areas:

### Supplier Strategy

Choose between:

- Single Supplier
- Multiple Suppliers

Learn the trade-off between cost efficiency and supply chain resilience.

---

### Factory Location

Select the best manufacturing location based on:

- Cost
- Risk
- Labor Availability
- Market Proximity

---

### Warehouse Strategy

Plan inventory distribution through:

- Centralized Warehousing
- Regional Warehouses
- Direct-to-Customer Model

Understand the balance between operational cost and delivery performance.

---

### Transportation Strategy

Choose transportation methods:

- 🚚 Road
- 🚆 Rail
- 🚢 Sea
- ✈️ Air

Each option affects:

- Delivery Speed
- Cost
- Sustainability
- Risk

---

### Inventory Strategy

Decide between:

- Low Inventory
- Balanced Inventory
- High Inventory

Learn how inventory impacts customer satisfaction, working capital, and resilience.
