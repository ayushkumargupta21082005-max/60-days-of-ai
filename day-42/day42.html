❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Q: How much should I save as a student?
A: Aim to save 10-20% of your income if possible. Even ₹1000-2000 per month adds up over time. Start small and increase gradually.
Q: Is it too early to start investing?
A: No! Starting early gives you time to benefit from compound growth. Consider starting with low-risk options like savings accounts or government schemes.
Q: How to handle irregular income from part-time work?
A: Calculate your average monthly income and budget conservatively based on that. Any extra income can go directly to savings or goals.
Q: What's the 50/30/20 budgeting rule?
A: Allocate 50% to needs (rent, food, utilities), 30% to wants (entertainment, dining), and 20% to savings. Adjust based on your priorities.
